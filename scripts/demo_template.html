How it works

A LightGBM model trained on __NTRAIN__ Athens entire homes and private rooms runs in your browser. Tested on hosts it never saw (five-fold cross-validation grouped by host), its typical miss is about __MDAPE__% of the asking price. The ranges above come from those misses, scaled to your estimate. Hosts with identical homes often ask different prices, so some spread is unavoidable.

What it is not

Asking prices, not booked prices. One summer snapshot, so no seasonality. Location effects are associations, not causes. Very cheap and very expensive homes are predicted least well.
